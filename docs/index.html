02 / HOW I WORK

A practical approach
to software quality.

Testing is more than checking whether something works once. It's understanding the expectation, finding meaningful risks, and communicating the result.

01◎

Understand

Break down requirements, clarify expected behavior, and trace what needs coverage.

02⌘

Exercise

Use manual, automated, API, negative, and boundary checks to test real behavior.

03◇

Investigate

Reproduce failures, separate test issues from product defects, and document useful evidence.

04✓

Verify

Retest repairs, protect existing functionality, and communicate what has actually passed.

A WORKFLOW BUILT AROUND ACCOUNTABILITY

AI helps scale the work.
Human judgment keeps it honest.

On Ark of Amerigo, I use ChatGPT-assisted planning and an inline coding agent, with Jira for tracking and GitHub for source and evidence. I remain responsible for architecture decisions, priorities, quality criteria, and acceptance. The goal is a stronger process, not inflated claims about who wrote the code.
